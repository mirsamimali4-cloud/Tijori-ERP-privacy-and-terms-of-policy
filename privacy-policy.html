Privacy Policy

Tijori Jewellery ERP · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Tijori Jewellery ERP ("Tijori") handles information when you use the Windows desktop application, including its optional Google Drive backup feature.

Who operates Tijori

Tijori is operated by MS Globe Software and Solutions. For privacy questions or requests, contact mirsamimali4@gmail.com.

Information stored on your computer

Tijori stores its business database on your Windows computer. Depending on how you use the app, it can contain inventory, purchases, sales, supplier and customer records, and related business details. The database is managed locally by the app and is not sent to Tijori's operator as part of normal use. Keep your computer and local database protected.

Optional Google sign-in and Drive backups

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Tijori uses Google sign-in to receive your Google account email address and authorization tokens. The email address is shown in the app to identify the connected account. Tokens are stored on your computer using Windows secure storage.

When you create a backup, Tijori uploads a snapshot of the app's database to a Tijori Backups folder in your Google Drive. A database snapshot may include the business and personal information described above. Tijori uses Google's Drive API with the drive.file permission for the files it creates or uses for this feature. Backups are stored in your Google account and count toward your Drive storage. Google handles the sign-in and Drive data under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Tijori creates a backup when you request one and may create one automatically while the app is running, no more often than once every 12 hours. It keeps the 30 newest Tijori backup files; older Tijori backups may be deleted automatically. Restoring a backup replaces the app's current database with the selected snapshot. Tijori also saves a local safety copy before completing a restore.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive in Tijori's Cloud Backup screen. Tijori asks Google to revoke the saved authorization token and removes that token from this computer. Disconnecting does not delete backups already stored in Google Drive. To remove those, sign in to Google Drive and delete the files in the Tijori Backups folder yourself.

You can also delete local business records from within the app. Local database and restore safety-copy files are stored on your computer; uninstalling Tijori may not remove them. Use Windows or your backup tools to remove them if you no longer need them.

Information shared with other services

For the optional backup feature, information is sent directly between Tijori on your computer and Google services to sign you in and manage your backups. Tijori's operator does not receive the database snapshots or Google tokens through an app-operated server. If live bullion rates are configured, Tijori also requests rate data from GoldAPI.io; that service may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Review the privacy terms of Google and GoldAPI.io for their handling of information.

Tijori does not sell your information. It does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the backup and restore feature.

Security

Google authorization tokens are protected with Windows secure storage on the computer where you connect the account. Backups in Google Drive are subject to the security and account settings of your Google account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so use a strong Windows sign-in and Google account protections, and keep separate backups of important business data.

Children

Tijori is a business application and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Tijori's features or data practices change. The current version and its revision date will be published at the privacy policy address provided in the app's Google OAuth configuration.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact mirsamimali4@gmail.com.
