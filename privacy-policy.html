Privacy Policy

Tijori Jewellery ERP · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Tijori Jewellery ERP ("Tijori") handles information when you use the Windows desktop application.

Who operates Tijori

Tijori is operated by MS Globe Software and Solutions. For privacy questions or requests, contact mirsamimali4@gmail.com.

Information stored on your computer

Tijori stores its business database on your Windows computer. Depending on how you use the app, it can contain inventory, purchases, sales, supplier and customer records, and related business details. The database is managed locally by the app and is not sent to Tijori's operator as part of normal use. Keep your computer and local database protected.

No account or cloud sign-in

Tijori does not require an account and currently has no Google sign-in or cloud backup feature. The app does not upload your business database to Tijori's operator. Each Windows user has a separate local database stored in that user's application data folder. Updates preserve the existing database; a new installation creates an empty one.

Deleting data

You can delete business records from within the app. The local database is stored on your computer; uninstalling Tijori may not remove it. Use Windows or your backup tools to remove the database or any copies if you no longer need them.

Information shared with other services

If live bullion rates are configured, Tijori requests rate data from GoldAPI.io; that service may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Review GoldAPI.io's privacy terms for its handling of information. No business database records are included in that rate request.

Tijori's operator does not receive your local database through an app-operated server and does not sell your information.

Security

Your business database is stored locally on your Windows computer. Protect access to your Windows account and keep separate backups of important business data. No method of storage is completely secure.

Children

Tijori is a business application and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Tijori's features or data practices change. The current version and its revision date will be published at this address.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact mirsamimali4@gmail.com.
